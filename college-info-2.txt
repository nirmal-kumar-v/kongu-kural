## 1.3 Frequently Asked Questions & Answers — Hostels

Q: What are the hostel options and blocks available at KEC?
A: KEC provides separate residential hostel blocks for boys and girls. Major men's hostels include Dheeran, Valluvar, and Kaveri blocks. Major women's hostels include Amaravathi and Bhavani blocks.

Q: Who is the Chief Warden and how can they be contacted?
A: Dr. R. Parameshwaran serves as the Chief Warden (Contact: 9842722655, email: principal@kongu.ac.in).

Q: Who is the Deputy Warden for Amaravathi hostel?
A: Ms. D. Ramya is the Deputy Warden for Amaravathi hostel (Mobile: 9789397940).

Q: What are the annual hostel fees at KEC?
A: Annual hostel fees generally range between ₹50,000 and ₹80,000 per academic year, covering room accommodation and boarding depending on room type and amenities.

## 2.4 Head of the Institution / Profile of the Principal — Dr. R. Parameshwaran

Name: Dr. R. Parameshwaran, M.E., Ph.D.
Designation: Principal, Kongu Engineering College (KEC)
Role: Head of the Institution

Professional Summary & Academic Qualifications:
Dr. R. Parameshwaran is a distinguished academician, prolific researcher, and innovation-driven educator with 25 years of experience in Engineering Education and Applied Research.
• Undergraduate Degree: B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Madras University.
• Postgraduate Degree: M.E. in Industrial Engineering from NIT Trichy (National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli).
• Doctoral Degree: Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Anna University, Chennai.
• Research Domains: New Product Development, Quality Engineering, Intelligent Systems, and Advanced Manufacturing Processes.

Research Publications & Funded Projects:
• Journal Publications: 65 research papers in reputed international journals, including 45 SCI-indexed publications.
• Conference Presentations: Over 70 papers presented at national and international conferences.
• Book Chapters: Authored 4 book chapters.
• Funded R&D Projects: Successfully completed 7 funded R&D projects with a financial outlay of Rs. 2.86 crores from leading bodies including AICTE, UGC, DST, and MSME, with the most notable being the ongoing DST-funded project on "Development of an intelligent powder-based 3D metal printer".
• Doctoral Guidance: Guided 10 Ph.D. scholars under Anna University and continues to mentor researchers in composite materials, welding, and optimization.
• Industry Consultancy: Executed 11 consultancy projects and organized 8 funded programs along with several national and international conferences.

Leadership & Administrative Roles at KEC:
• Head of Mechatronics Department (2010–2019): Spearheaded academic, research, and industry-aligned initiatives.
• Head of Technology Business Incubator (TBI@KEC) (Since 2020): Secured funding under prestigious national schemes including NIDHI-PRAYAS, NIDHI-EIR, and the MSME Incubation Scheme, attracting over ₹7.5 crores of grants and supporting more than 60 start-ups.
• President of the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) (Since 2018): Dynamic leader in innovation and entrepreneurship development; Certified Innovation Ambassador.
• Head of Placement and Training Cell (Since 2021): Under his leadership, nearly 300 companies conduct recruitment drives annually, resulting in an average of 1,250 student placements each year.

Honours & Accolades:
• Best Faculty Award
• Best Performing Placement Team Award
• Multiple Best Department Awards for academic and industry-linked excellence
• Certified Innovation Ambassador

## 8.5 Campus Wi-Fi Network Registration (SSID: Kongu_Wifi) & Computing Facilities

Title: Official Wi-Fi Registration Form & Campus Wireless Access Procedures
Institution: Kongu Engineering College (Autonomous), Perundurai - 638 060
Department: Department of Computer Science and Engineering / Campus-Wide Computing Facilities
Network SSID: Kongu_Wifi
Firewall Authentication: Netid

1. Overview and Purpose:
Kongu Engineering College provides secure campus-wide wireless internet access under the SSID "Kongu_Wifi". Wireless access is provided strictly for academic work, research, and learning purposes. Students (including first-year / fresher students joining KEC and senior students) and faculty members who wish to connect their personal laptops or desktops to the campus network must register their devices using the official Wi-Fi Registration Form.

2. Official Wi-Fi Registration Form Structure:
The official KEC Wi-Fi Registration Form is divided into two primary parts:
Part I. General Information:
• Faculty / Roll Number
• Student Name
• Sex: Male / Female
• Course & Branch (e.g., B.E. CSE, B.Tech IT, Mech, etc.)
• Course Duration: From (Year) To (Year)
• Hostel / Room No. (for hostellers)
• Official e-mail ID

Part II. Technical Information:
• Type of Device: Desktop / Laptop
• Device Make, Model & Serial Number
• Wireless Device MAC Address: 6-octet physical hexadecimal hardware address (Example: 01 23 89 AB CD EF)
• Operating System installed and Version (Example: Windows, Mac, and Linux)
• Antivirus Product: Specify the active antivirus product installed on the device

3. Student Declaration & Terms of Access:
Students must sign the declaration stating:
"I hereby declare that the above information given by me is true and correct. I understand that the Wireless access is provided to me for my academic work only. I also agree that I will not share this account with any person and will take due care in protecting it."

4. Required Signatures and Verification:
To complete registration, the form must be signed by:
• Student Signature with Date
• Deputy Warden (for Hostellers) OR Class Advisor (for Day Scholars only)
• Head of the Department (HOD)

5. Network Login Credentials & Firewall Authentication:
• Username and Password are the same as used for Firewall Authentication (Netid) and Kongu_Wifi.
• Step-by-step connection tutorials and configuration guides are available at: https://kongu.edu/wifi/index.html

6. First-Year Students (Freshers) Onboarding & Campus Wi-Fi Guidance:
• When joining KEC, first-year students need to complete basic campus onboarding and registration procedures.
• To access internet on laptops/desktops for academic and lab coursework, first-year students must download and submit the official KEC Wi-Fi Registration Form.
• Students fill in their Roll Number, hostel room (if hosteller), laptop model, MAC address, OS, and antivirus details.
• Day scholars obtain approval from their Class Advisor, while hostellers obtain signature from their Deputy Warden, followed by the HOD's approval.
• After submission to the computing centre / network administration office, the student's device MAC address and Netid are registered for Kongu_Wifi access.

Frequently Asked Questions:
Q: How do I get campus Wi-Fi at KEC?
A: Download the official KEC Wi-Fi Registration Form, fill in your roll number, device type (laptop/desktop), MAC address, OS, and antivirus details, get signature from your Class Advisor (for day scholars) or Deputy Warden (for hostellers) along with your HOD, and submit it. Your Netid and firewall credentials will grant access to SSID "Kongu_Wifi".

Q: Where can I download the KEC Wi-Fi registration form?
A: The official KEC Wi-Fi Registration Form PDF is available for direct download via the "Download Wi-Fi Registration Form" button in Kongu Kural or from the campus computing portal.

Q: What is the campus Wi-Fi network SSID?
A: The campus wireless network SSID is "Kongu_Wifi".

Q: What are the credentials for logging into Kongu_Wifi?
A: The username and password are the same as your Firewall Authentication (Netid). Tutorials are accessible at https://kongu.edu/wifi/index.html.
